*CUSTOMER WHATSAPP BOT FLOW*

1. Customer official WhatsApp number par message karega.

2. Bot customer ko welcome message bhejega.

3. Bot customer se naam puchega.

4. Bot customer se city/area puchega.

5. Bot customer profile create karega and wallet initialize karega.

6. Bot main menu show karega:
   - Browse Offers
   - My Wallet
   - Recharge Wallet
   - My History
   - Help

7. Customer Browse Offers click karega.

8. Bot categories show karega.

9. Customer category choose karega.

10. Bot available offers list bhejega.

11. Customer offer claim karega.

12. Wallet se deduction hoga.

13. Unique coupon code generate hoga.

14. Coupon WhatsApp par customer ko send hoga.

15. Customer vendor store par coupon redeem karega.

16. Vendor app se coupon verify karega.

17. Bot redemption confirmation bhejega.

18. Customer anytime wallet balance ya history dekh sakega.

19. Customer recharge karega if wallet low.
